Fast Download Manager (FDM) is a modern, high-performance Android download manager and media browser built with Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Kotlin Coroutines.

Features:
- Streamlined Downloads Dashboard: Real-time speed & ETA, category filters (Videos, Music, Documents, Archives, Apps).
- Multi-Threaded Engine: Concurrent chunked downloads configurable from 1 to 32 connections.
- Integrated Media Sniffer Browser: In-app browser that automatically detects downloadable video and audio streams (.m3u8, .mpd, .mp4, .mp3).
- Material 3 & Edge-to-Edge Design: Modern dynamic colors, adaptive dark theme, and smooth animations.
- Privacy & Controls: Wi-Fi only download restriction toggle and built-in privacy settings.
- 100% Free & Open Source: No ads, no tracking, built with modern open-source Kotlin libraries.
